## One thing to check before subscribing to any IPTV service

“IPTV” describes the way television is delivered over the internet. It does not, by itself, tell you whether a particular provider has the right to distribute every channel or event it offers.

The U.S. Copyright Office notes that unauthorized retransmission of copyrighted broadcasts over the internet can implicate copyright rights.

For college football, that means it is worth verifying the provider's authorization for the content you intend to watch rather than assuming that a large channel number automatically means official access.
